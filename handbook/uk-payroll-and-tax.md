# UK payroll and tax

Figures our finance lead uses when budgeting for staff. All figures are for the United Kingdom.

The National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £11.44 an hour.

Employers pay National Insurance at a rate of 13.8% on earnings above the secondary threshold.

The Bank of England base rate is 5.25%.

The standard rate of VAT is 20%.

The main rate of corporation tax is 25%.

The income tax personal allowance is £12,570 a year.
